(03) — Questions
What is MtaaLink?
MtaaLink is an all-in-one management platform for Kenyan organizations. Members, money, meetings, projects, elections and SMS announcements — all in one place, running in any browser.
Who is it for?
Any organization that manages members and money — chamas, churches, SACCOs, welfare groups, villages, and small businesses.
How much does MtaaLink cost?
Free during early access. When paid plans launch, MtaaLink will cost KES 2,500 per month — one plan, unlimited members, SMS included.
Do I need to install an app?
No. MtaaLink runs in your browser on any phone. You can optionally install it to your home screen for faster access — but it's not required.
Can I run elections in MtaaLink?
Yes. Create an election, we send unique voting codes to all members via SMS, and results are tallied live. Members can vote by replying to the SMS or on our secure web page — no app needed.
Can I send SMS announcements to members?
Yes. Send announcements to all members or specific groups by SMS. Track delivery, and reach members on any phone — no smartphone or app required.
Can I export my data?
Yes. Every report can be exported to PDF in one click — member lists, contributions, expenses, election results, meeting minutes.
How do I get started?
Sign up free at mtaalink.org/register. Takes 2 minutes. You'll be managing your organization the same day.
